# Selecting a useful focus cue

**English** | [简体中文](guidance-2026-10-26.zh-CN.md)

The next experiment evaluates the final selected candidate, alongside the
historical per-candidate box and eye metrics. A box can have poor IoU while
still locating the bird. Conversely, a point inside a bird box can lie on a
foreground branch. Geometry is a development proxy; visible surface, depth,
designated bird identity and camera focus are not established by these labels.

## Metrics and partial labels

`bird_guidance_metrics.py` reports selected-eye hits within 2/4/8 input pixels,
head-region and bird-region cues, points outside labelled geometry, unknown
regions and abstentions. Unused duplicate candidates do not count as selected
eye errors. Missing eye labels remain unknown. Traditional candidate metrics
are retained to expose duplicate detections and box differences.

An eye more than 4 pixels from the labelled eye may provide a coarse head cue;
it never becomes a precise eye hit merely by falling inside a head box.
Outside-geometry selections are unmatched proxies, not proven branch locks.

For the fixed n parent at eye threshold .8, with native B/H and batch one,
the 80-image development set has 62 geometrically supported selections,
15 selected-eye hits within 4 pixels, 2 unmatched selected eyes on the fixed
26-image eye-assessed subset, and 18 selected eyes on unassessed images.
The labelled-eye denominator is 25 images; this is distinct from counting all
eye points or all emitted candidates. These are assisted, repeatedly used
development labels, not an independent camera evaluation.

The policy search uses the same eye threshold grid for every candidate and
either no eye gate or a predicted-head/high-confidence gate. It protects the
parent's geometric coverage, conditional eye error count, unknown-eye
promotion and explicit absence result. Full curves remain available. No new
independent event data have been received.

## Training design

`train_bird_guidance.py` continues the same parent with the frozen 375 community
and 13,958 public training records; the 80 community development records select
epochs and thresholds. Historical tests are excluded. Conditions are ordinary
continuation, localization-quality eye confidence, and that confidence plus
mild whole-image blur, each at two sampling seeds. Same-seed sample order is
hashed. Every epoch and separate guidance, eye and traditional optima are saved.

Quality confidence uses a detached localization-dependent soft target; eye
coordinate regression and B/H detection losses are retained. In the blur arm,
affected eye labels become unknown and their original regions are protected.
Blurred pixels are synthetic appearance augmentation, not physically correct
depth-dependent defocus. No production annotations are rewritten.

The implementation uses the pinned existing local conda environment. Sony,
remote/A800 work and camera deployment remain paused.

## Completed results

All six matched runs completed 8 epochs each, taking 27.49 minutes in the formal
training loops. Each retained the parent under the frozen fine-guidance rule.
All 48 epoch weights were then replayed uniformly, with separate post-hoc
module-combination and acquisition-proxy analyses.

| Development policy | Geometric support /80 | Selected eyes within 4px /25 labelled images | Unmatched selected eyes | Selected eyes without eye truth |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Native B/H + parent n eyes | 62 | 15 | 2 | 18 |
| Two n models, separate B/H and eye weights | 68 | 15 | 2 | 18 |
| Previous hard-context weight, coarse acquisition threshold | 71 | 14 | 11 | 46 |
| New quality + blur weight, coarse acquisition threshold | 70 | 18 | 7 | 51 |

Unmatched-eye counts are conditional on the same 26 eye-assessed images, not a
whole-image false-lock rate. All four policies made no selection on the single
explicitly absent image. The two-model combination had 1 selected point outside
labelled geometry, compared with 6 for the native-B/H parent policy. The old and
new coarse candidates had 3 and 6 respectively. No additional human truth was
created from these geometry checks.

The two-model candidate reuses the previous baseline's B/H and the retained
parent's eyes; it is not a newly successful single-network fine-tune. Actual
80-image inference reproduced the cached counts. Warm PNG read, preprocessing,
resident model(s), decoding and selection had P50/P95 of 25.94/32.56 ms for one
n and 75.45/110.05 ms for two n, 18 samples per variant. This excludes model
construction and does not measure the camera NPU or lens.

The new coarse candidate is epoch 7 of the second quality-blur seed, at eye
threshold .2; the old coarse reference uses .4. These thresholds belong to
different weights and are not directly comparable confidence scales. Regrading
the old weights also improves their coarse scores: comparing new loose results
only with old strict results would overstate training improvement. Coarse
positions remain review candidates, not verified eye locks.

## Diagnostic findings

The community train partition contains 56 conservative association groups;
the largest holds 132/375 images. Validation contains 6 groups. Association
groups are not necessarily individual capture events. The old two-model
combination improved geometry in two groups; group-macro support changed from
85.42% to 90.00%, still on repeatedly used development data.

Restoring parent BatchNorm buffers while holding every learned tensor fixed
changed predictions in both directions and did not yield a passing new
checkpoint. It does not establish BN as the sole regression cause. Training
updates were verified; retaining epoch zero does not mean optimization never ran.

Cached teacher calibration used 200 training images. A universal .95 cutoff
left zero corroborated head candidates, so confidence thresholds need per-part
calibration. Unknown parts and missed detections never become negatives. No
extra distillation arm was added to the frozen matrix.

An 18-image availability queue asks whether an unlabelled predicted eye is
visible and whether its point provides a usable capture cue. It contains
unreviewed proposals only and changes no production labels. Independent new
event/burst data remain unavailable; Sony and camera deployment remain paused.

The first pilot had a JSON tuple/list readback check bug; a first matrix was
stopped after three epochs when auxiliary protection incorrectly overrode
positive box geometry. Both failed/interrupted records were retained. The six
reported runs use the corrected frozen implementation. 120 tests passed.
